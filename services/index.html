Everything else we treat

The pages above cover the treatments people research most before booking. The rest of our work falls into these areas — all of it available at the Sion clinic.

Check-ups, cleaning and X-rays

A routine examination, scaling and polishing to remove plaque and tartar, and digital X-rays where we need to see below the gum line. This is the appointment to book if nothing hurts and you simply want things checked, or if your gums bleed when you brush.

Fillings

Tooth-coloured composite fillings for decay caught before it reaches the nerve. If decay has already reached the nerve, a filling won't be enough — that's when root canal treatment comes in.

Crowns and bridges

A crown caps a tooth that is heavily filled, cracked, or has just had a root canal. A bridge replaces a missing tooth by anchoring to the teeth on either side. Both are made in a range of materials — metal-ceramic through to full zirconia — and we'll explain the trade-off between strength, appearance and cost before you decide.

Extractions and minor oral surgery

Removing teeth that can't be saved, including wisdom teeth that are impacted or repeatedly getting infected. Straightforward extractions are done under local anaesthetic in a single visit.

Teeth whitening and cosmetic dentistry

In-clinic whitening for teeth that have darkened with age, coffee, tea or tobacco. Also veneers and composite bonding for chipped, worn or uneven front teeth. Whitening works on natural tooth structure — it won't change the colour of existing crowns, veneers or fillings, so those sometimes need replacing afterwards to match.

Dentures

Complete and partial removable dentures where implants or bridges aren't practical or affordable. Dentures can also be made to clip onto implants, which stops the lower denture moving while you eat.

In pain right now? Call +91 90827 27843 rather than booking online. Swelling, a broken tooth or throbbing pain that keeps you awake should be seen the same day if possible.
