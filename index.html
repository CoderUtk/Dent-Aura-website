What we treat

Every treatment, grouped the way you'd search for it

From a routine check-up to a full smile makeover — here's what's available at Dent-Aura. Not sure which category you need? Message us on WhatsApp and describe what's bothering you.

Check-ups & X-rays Fillings & Root Canal Crowns & Bridges Extractions & Surgery Braces & Aligners Whitening & Cosmetic Dentures & Implants Skin & Aesthetics

Diagnostic & Preventive

Catch problems early, before they turn into expensive ones.

  • Routine check-ups
  • Digital X-rays
  • Fluoride application
  • Scaling & polishing (cleaning)

Fillings & Root Canal

For cavities, tooth pain, and saving a tooth instead of losing it.

  • Fillings — temporary, GIC & composite
  • Anterior composite build-up
  • Root canal treatment (RCT)
  • Pulpectomy, post & core

Crowns & Bridges

Rebuild a broken or heavily treated tooth so it looks and works like new.

  • Metal & PFM crowns
  • DMLS crowns
  • Zirconia crowns, multiple warranty options
  • Crown cementation

Extractions & Oral Surgery

For teeth that can't be saved, wisdom teeth, and minor surgical procedures.

  • Simple & surgical extractions
  • Complicated & disimpaction cases
  • Frenectomy
  • Flap surgery

Braces & Aligners

Straighten crooked or crowded teeth, at any age.

  • Metallic & ceramic braces
  • Retainers
  • Nightguards

Whitening & Cosmetic

For a brighter smile or a little extra sparkle.

  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Tooth jewellery

Dentures & Implants

For replacing missing teeth — from a single tooth to a full set.

  • Removable partial dentures (RPD)
  • Complete dentures
  • Dental implants

Not listed here doesn't mean we don't do it — full pricing and anything else you're wondering about is one WhatsApp message away.

Skin & Aesthetics

Aesthetic care beyond the smile

Alongside dental care, Dent-Aura offers a set of skin and hair aesthetic treatments for patients looking to extend their care beyond the chair.

Hair PRP Therapy

Platelet-rich plasma treatment to support hair density and scalp health.

GFC Hair Treatment

Growth Factor Concentrate therapy to help strengthen and restore hair.

Exo-GFC Advanced Therapy

An advanced exosome-enhanced treatment for more targeted hair restoration support.

Chemical Peels

Skin resurfacing peels to help even out tone and refresh texture.

Microneedling

An anti-ageing treatment that stimulates collagen for firmer, smoother-looking skin.

Glutathione Drips

IV drips aimed at supporting skin brightening and overall antioxidant support.

Medifacials

Clinical, dermatologist-style facials tailored to your skin's specific needs.

Laser Facials

Laser-based treatments to help refine texture, tone and overall skin clarity.

Laser Hair Reduction

A longer-term solution for reducing unwanted body and facial hair.

Led by Dr. Jyoti Gupta, who holds a PG Diploma in Cosmetology, Trichology and Lasers in Skin Aesthetics — and more available in-clinic. Ask our team or message us on WhatsApp about the full range.
