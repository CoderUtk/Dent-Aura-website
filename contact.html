Getting here

By train

Sion is on the Central Line. Come out of the station and the clinic is about a two-minute walk in Sindhi Colony — look for McDonald's, we're right next to it. If you're coming from the Harbour Line, GTB Nagar and Kurla both connect through easily.

By road

We're just off the Sion Circle junction, which puts us within easy reach of Dadar, Matunga, Wadala, Chembur and Kurla. Coming down the Eastern Express Highway, exit at Sion and head toward the station.

Parking

Street parking around Sindhi Colony fills up, particularly during evening clinic hours. If you're driving in the evening, allow a few extra minutes. Auto-rickshaws from anywhere in Sion, Matunga or Dadar are usually the simpler option.

Which appointment should you book?

If you're not sure, message us on WhatsApp and describe the problem — a photo helps. Broadly:

  • Pain, swelling or a broken tooth — call rather than message, and say it's urgent. We'll fit you in the same day where we can.
  • Nothing hurts, just due a check — book a routine examination and cleaning.
  • Thinking about straightening your teeth — book an orthodontic assessment, which includes X-rays.
  • Missing a tooth — book an implant consultation so we can check the bone before discussing options.
  • Want to know costs first — read how our fees work, then ask us for a ballpark.

In pain outside clinic hours? Call +91 90827 27843 and leave a message, or send a WhatsApp. For facial swelling with difficulty swallowing or breathing, go to a hospital emergency department — that can't wait for morning.

Before your first visit

  • Bring any previous X-rays, treatment plans or quotes you have
  • Bring a list of medicines you take, including blood thinners and diabetes medication
  • Tell us about heart conditions, joint replacements, or any allergy to anaesthetic or antibiotics
  • If you're anxious about dental treatment, say so when you book — it genuinely changes how we approach the appointment
